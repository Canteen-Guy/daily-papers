### 1. **[Cu intercalation and field-driven filament formation in vertically aligned MoS$_2$ memristors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37152v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 82/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-27
- **作者**: Touko Lehenkari, Hannu-Pekka Komsa
- **标签**: `cond-mat.mes-hall` `cond-mat.mtrl-sci`
- **AI摘要**: 揭示MoS2忆阻器铜丝形成机制
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Vertically-aligned MoS$_2$ films paired with a Cu top electrode have been used as electrochemical metallization cell memristor devices. The memristive switching has been attributed to the formation of conductive filaments, yet their exact structure has remained elusive. Here we carry out a multi-scale computational study of Cu intercalation, clustering, and field-driven dynamics to eventually model the filament formation under operating conditions. We use density-functional theory results to fine-tune a machine learning force field, and the most important physical phenomena are further distilled into multi-particle kinetic Monte Carlo simulations. By exhaustive enumeration of Cu intercalated structures, we find the most stable structure at MoS$_2$Cu$_{0.11}$ composition, with Cu atoms forming a dice lattice between the MoS$_2$ layers. These structures are shown to exhibit metallic character and can thus form conductive filaments under memristor operation. The simulated switching speed and threshold voltage agree well with the available experimental data. Our results give fundamental insights on the device operation mechanism and guidelines for maximizing switching speed and endurance.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 多尺度模拟揭示了铜插层与导电丝形成的微观机理

### 2. **[Electric Field-Induced Hole- and Electron-Type Flat Bands in Twisted Double Bilayer Graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37402v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 78/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-27
- **作者**: Zhihao Jiang, Dongkyu Lee, Alfred J. H. Jones et al.
- **标签**: `cond-mat.mes-hall` `cond-mat.mtrl-sci`
- **AI摘要**: 揭示扭转双层石墨烯电场诱导平带效应
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Application of a finite electric field and small twist angle between two graphene bilayers leads to widely tunable electronic structures that support emergent phenomena. Here, we measure the electronic structure of twisted double bilayer graphene with twist angles of 3.1$^{\circ}$ and 6.0$^{\circ}$ using micro-focused angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (microARPES) on \textit{in situ} gated samples. At a twist angle of 6.0$^{\circ}$, we find that the dispersion around the valence and conduction band extrema is unchanged compared to separate graphene bilayers, whereas electric field tunable hybridisation gaps and band flattening effects occur at higher binding energies. In the 3.1$^{\circ}$ structure, we find that the resulting superlattice drives dispersion changes around the valence and conduction band edges. Increasing the gate-induced doping in the bottom bilayer leads to the formation of flat bands between the valence band maximum in the bottom bilayer and conduction band minimum in the top bilayer. Our experiments establish combinations of twist angle and electric field that potentially support strong electron-hole excitations and that provide a benchmark for the theoretical description of moderately twisted double bilayer graphene.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 实验设计严谨，通过微区ARPES揭示了能带调控机制。

### 3. **[Surface-Illuminated Interdigitated Ge-on-Si Avalanche Photodiode With Separated Ge Absorption, Si Multiplication, and Lateral Field Engineering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37593v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 76/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-27
- **作者**: Mason Clewis, Kuang-Yun Tu, Ching-Yu Hsu et al.
- **标签**: `physics.optics`
- **AI摘要**: 提出侧向Ge-on-Si雪崩光电二极管设计
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

This work presents a surface-illuminated lateral Ge-on-Si interdigitated separate-absorption-charge-multiplication (SACM) avalanche photodiode for 1550-nm detection. The intrinsic-region spacing, P$^-$ charge-control width, and finger geometry are engineered to suppress high-field penetration toward the Ge/Si heterointerface and localize avalanche multiplication in Si. Simulations show that wider charge-control or intrinsic regions reduce fixed-bias dark current and shift avalanche onset to higher reverse bias. Among the tested structures, the intrinsic-region spacing/P$^-$ charge-control width of 0.5/0.15 $μ$m achieved a peak responsivity of 11.81 A/W at 1550 nm. At a -20 V applied voltage, it exhibited a multiplication gain of 3.796, a 3-dB bandwidth of 7.13 GHz, and a matched-bias gain-bandwidth product of 27.07 GHz. Optical-power-dependent measurements also show avalanche-gain compression consistent with space-charge screening. These results demonstrate effective lateral-field engineering for high-speed, surface-illuminated Ge-on-Si APDs.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 通过侧向场工程优化了光电性能，实验数据详实。

### 4. **[First-Principles Investigation of Irida-Graphane: A Wide-Band-Gap Semiconductor Derived from Irida-Graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37095v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 75/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-27
- **作者**: Djardiel S. Gomes, Isaac M. Félix, Marcelo L. Pereira Junior
- **标签**: `cond-mat.mtrl-sci`
- **AI摘要**: 提出并验证了宽带隙半导体铱达石墨烷
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Opening a band gap in metallic two-dimensional carbon allotropes is an important step toward their use in electronic and optoelectronic devices, and full hydrogenation has proven to be an effective route to achieve it. Here, we propose irida-graphane, the fully hydrogenated derivative of the irida-graphene, a monolayer composed of three-, six-, and eight-membered rings, and investigate its structural, mechanical, electronic, and optical properties using density functional theory (DFT) at the PBE and HSE06 levels combined with phonon and \textit{ab initio} molecular dynamics (AIMD) simulations. All carbon atoms adopt $sp^3$ hybridization, with the hydrogen atoms of the three- and six-membered rings bonded to opposite sides of a buckled carbon framework, and the lattice parameter contracts by 4.3% with respect to irida-graphene. The hydrogenation energy of $-0.53$ eV/H indicates that the hydrogenation of irida-graphene from molecular hydrogen is exothermic. The absence of imaginary phonon frequencies confirms the dynamical stability of irida-graphane, and AIMD simulations show that its structure remains intact at temperatures up to 2000 K. Irida-graphane is mechanically isotropic, with an in-plane stiffness of approximately 156 N/m, a Poisson's ratio of approximately 0.1, and critical strains of approximately 20%. Hydrogenation transforms the metallic parent structure into a semiconductor with an indirect band gap of 4.68 eV (PBE) and 6.0 eV (HSE06). Irida-graphane is transparent in the visible range, with reflectivity below 0.3%, and absorbs strongly in the deep ultraviolet, with absorption coefficients of the order of $10^{5} - 10^{6}$ cm$^{-1}$. These results suggest irida-graphane as a candidate wide-gap material for ultraviolet optoelectronics and transparent coatings.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 通过DFT计算证实了材料的稳定性与光电潜力

### 5. **[Prussian blue and Prussian white thin-film electrodes: Single-step electrodeposition for anode and cathode applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37023v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 74/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-27
- **作者**: Michael Pohlitz, Mohammed Fayis Kalady, Larissa de O. Garcia et al.
- **标签**: `cond-mat.mtrl-sci`
- **AI摘要**: 通过电沉积调控普鲁士蓝薄膜成分与结构
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

This study examines how deposition potential influences the composition and nanoscale structure of Prussian Blue (PB)/Prussian White (PW)-family films, providing an experimental basis for assessing their proposed functions. Films were grown directly on Au/Cr/Si in a single potentiostatic step from a common aqueous bath at $-0.20$ to $+0.40$\,V versus a saturated calomel electrode, with a fixed deposition charge of 10\,mC. Electron microscopy, elemental analysis, X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy, and Raman spectroscopy revealed potential-dependent chemistry and nonmonotonic morphology. EDS indicated greater K incorporation in lower-potential deposits, whose Fe~2p and Raman features were consistent with more reduced, PW-like environments. Surface K/Fe ratios varied nonmonotonically from 1.129 to 1.322 across the series, corresponding to 52.95--56.94\% K within the detected K+Fe population. Larger faceted crystallites at $+0.30$\,V contrasted with finer pyramids and cracks at $+0.40$\,V. Selected PB-like and PW-like regions had local cubic lattice parameters of $(10.31\pm0.10)$\,Å and $(10.36\pm0.10)$\,Å, respectively; their overlapping uncertainties prevent discrimination by lattice spacing alone. Cross-sectional maps showed broadly uniform framework-element distributions within locally continuous coatings. Together, these observations relate deposition conditions to contrasting chemical and morphological starting states without establishing bulk stoichiometry or phase purity. A literature-grounded outlook identifies ion storage, sensing, electrochromism, resistive switching, and ion handling as research opportunities requiring functional validation in these films.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 实验方法严谨，系统揭示了沉积电位对薄膜性能的影响。
