### 1. **[Low magnon damping of a EuSe film down to millikelvin temperatures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21733v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 82/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-15
- **作者**: Rostyslav O. Serha, Jan Hofer, Khrystyna O. Levchenko et al.
- **标签**: `cond-mat.mtrl-sci` `cond-mat.mes-hall`
- **AI摘要**: EuSe薄膜在mK温区实现低磁振子阻尼
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

The rapid development of quantum magnonics over the past decade has created a demand for magnetic materials that keep their magnon losses low at millikelvin temperatures and add functionality beyond that of the established iron garnets and ferromagnetic metals. The europium chalcogenides are natural candidates $-$ they are magnetic semiconductors whose moment of $7\,μ_{\mathrm{B}}$ per Eu$^{2+}$ ion stems from a half-filled $4f$ shell with zero orbital momentum, so that spin-orbit coupling and intrinsic damping are expected to be weak. Here we characterize an epitaxial film of the metamagnet EuSe on diamagnetic BaF$_2$ by magnetometry and broadband ferromagnetic resonance from 10 K down to 30 mK. EuSe combines a saturation magnetization of 1100 kA/m with antiferromagnetic, ferrimagnetic and ferromagnetic phases that are reached with fields below 0.25 T. In contrast to a Gilbert-damped ferromagnet, the linewidth of the field-aligned film decreases with increasing frequency and field, and below 750 mK it no longer depends on temperature. At 30 mK, 68 GHz and 1.8 T, it reaches 5.5 mT, close to the values of bulk single crystals, which corresponds to a magnon lifetime of 2 ns and to an upper bound of $1.1\times10^{-3}$ for the Gilbert damping parameter. These properties make EuSe and the europium chalcogenides promising materials for hybrid quantum systems and quantum magnonics.
</details>

### 2. **[Bond-informed search for ordered configurations in multi-principal element alloys](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21718v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 82/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-15
- **作者**: Gaurav Arora, Alex Tai, Rodrigo Freitas et al.
- **标签**: `cond-mat.mtrl-sci`
- **AI摘要**: 提出MECx框架高效搜索多主元合金有序构型
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Ordered configurations in multi-principal element alloys (MPEAs) control their local structure and influence a broad range of mechanical and thermodynamic properties. Yet they are notoriously difficult to identify. Locating low-energy ordered candidates requires exploring a configurational space that grows combinatorially with the number of elements, and Monte Carlo searches driven by density-functional theory (DFT) need a new electronic-structure calculation at every trial step. Here we introduce MECx (Minimum Energy Configuration eXplorer), a framework that guides Monte Carlo searches with a surrogate energy model trained on configurations stratified in bond-count space. Applied to ternary, quaternary, and quinary BCC alloys using compact training sets of 100 DFT-labeled configurations, MECx identifies ordered configurations with lower energies than those found by direct DFT-based Monte Carlo within practical computational limits. These configurations exhibit distinct pair-specific ordering, including B2- and B32-like motifs, and undergo progressive disordering as temperature increases. The MPEA-trained models also transfer to selected binary subsystems without retraining. The resulting ordered configurations agree with those obtained by direct DFT-based Monte Carlo and produce experimentally distinguishable superlattice reflections in simulated neutron diffraction patterns.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 通过代理模型显著提升了合金构型搜索的效率与精度

### 3. **[Universal Dynamic Scaling for Sub-Yield Aging and Over-Yield Rejuvenation in A Cyclically Driven Metallic Glass](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21625v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 82/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-15
- **作者**: Nannan Ren, Hongfei Zhang, Rui Wan et al.
- **标签**: `cond-mat.soft` `cond-mat.mtrl-sci`
- **AI摘要**: 揭示金属玻璃循环形变动力学机制
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Whether sub-yield aging and over-yield rejuvenation in cyclically deformed amorphous solids share a common physical origin or arise from distinct microscopic mechanisms remains a fundamental yet unresolved debate. Using molecular dynamics simulations of a metallic glass, we resolve this duality by uncovering a unified dynamic clock that dictates energy landscape navigation across both regimes. We show that the transition between regimes reflects a fundamental shift in energy injection pathways: (i) In the over-yield regime, rejuvenation is driven as a first-order response to macroscopic plastic shear work derived from Herschel-Bulkley non-linear rheological scaling. (ii) In contrast, this macroscopic work scaling fails in the sub-yield regime, where early-stage energy landscape elevation and subsequent cyclic aging are instead governed by second-order localized elastic stress fluctuations acting as a mechanical catalyst and noise source, respectively. Intense high-rate agitation increases the escape probability from deeper potential energy minima, thereby suppressing the retention efficiency of structural relaxation. By identifying these localized fluctuations as the primary origin of rate-attenuated sub-yield relaxation, we establish an exact scaling identity relating the relaxation attenuation exponent to the stress fluctuation exponent. This work directly bridges non-linear rheology with stress fluctuation dynamics, providing a unified framework to predict and manipulate structural evolution in amorphous solids.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 通过分子动力学统一了亚屈服与过屈服机制，理论严谨。

### 4. **[Wavelength-selective bidirectional plasticity in self-powered Se/ZnMgO visual synapses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21618v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 82/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-15
- **作者**: Zacharie Jehl Li-Kao, Miho Akasaka, Myeongok Kim et al.
- **标签**: `cond-mat.mtrl-sci`
- **AI摘要**: 基于Se/ZnMgO的波长选择性自供电视觉突触
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Artificial visual synapses combine sensing, memory and processing in a single device, but most of them need an external voltage to read the synaptic weight and light is generally able to strengthen the weight rather than to weaken it. We report here Se/ZnMgO thin film photodiodes in which the photocurrent at zero bias writes and reads the synaptic weight, with a direction of change set by the photon energy alone. At equal photon flux, 365 nm pulses increase the photocurrent by 38 % while 455 nm pulses decrease it, and the depression reaching 30 % at higher flux, with up to 19 distinguishable levels per branch and no electrical energy spent. Spectrally resolved light soaking shows that short wavelengths potentiate while low-energy light erases. The interesting property is therefore the photon energy-dependent competition between potentiation and depression, the crossover being set by the defect population of the interface. Optical simulations show that both wavelengths are absorbed within the first tens of nanometres of Se, and the stored state is erased by a reverse bias in a voltage-dependent way and by sub-gap light, which indicates that potentiation corresponds to interface defects filled with electrons, in agreement with drift-diffusion simulations. The devices also show relearning, their response is modulated by bending on flexible substrates, and a network built from the measured weight updates reaches 89 % accuracy on handwritten digit recognition without noise and 85 % with the measured noise, against 97.5 % for floating-point weights.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 实现了无源光控突触权重调节，创新性与实用性兼备。

### 5. **[Interlocked Vortex Order in the Frustrated $A_3$V$_9$Te$_{13}$ Lattice ($A$=Cs,Rb)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21854v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 78/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-15
- **作者**: Zilong Li, H. -Q. Yuan, Xiaoqun Wang et al.
- **标签**: `cond-mat.str-el` `cond-mat.mtrl-sci`
- **AI摘要**: 揭示A3V9Te13中涡旋磁序与拓扑电子态的耦合机制
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Recently synthesized $A_3$V$_9$Te$_{13}$ ($A$ = Cs, Rb) provides a unique platform for exploring the interplay between geometric frustration, magnetism, and correlated electronic states beyond conventional kagome systems. Based on first-principles calculations, we demonstrate that the frustrated V sublattice naturally stabilizes a robust vortex or anti-vortex antiferromagnetic ground state, whose total energy is substantially lower than all other configurations. In addition, the Heisenberg model analysis show strong constraints on the ground state magnetic configuration, such that fixing spin configuration of 1 triangle propagates and fixes all others. The vortex or anti-vortex phase hosts electronic structures reminiscent of kagome metals, including Dirac-like band crossings, van Hove singularities, and flat bands near the Fermi level. Owing to the noncollinear spin texture and quasi-two-dimensional magnetic character, the Néel temperature exhibits an extremely weak dependence on both the magnitude and direction of external magnetic fields, consistent with experimental observations. For Cs-based compound, the ground state band structure is metallic with 6 Weyl points in the BZ; while for the Rb-based compound, spin--orbit coupling opens an approximately 27-meV gap at ambient pressure due to the lifted C$_3$ symmetry. Our results establish $A_3$V$_9$Te$_{13}$ as a promising platform for studying frustration-driven chiral magnetism and its interplay with topological electronic states.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 通过第一性原理计算阐明了挫折晶格中的磁性与拓扑特性
