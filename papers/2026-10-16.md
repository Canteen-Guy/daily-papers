- **AI摘要**: 利用腔体高度调控拓扑边缘态传输方向
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Topological edge states are a hallmark of topological phases, with their existence tied to bulk band topology through bulk-edge correspondence. In photonic pseudospin-Hall systems, these states exhibit pseudospin-momentum locking, with lattice interactions and interface geometry determining each pseudospin's propagation direction. Reversing the pseudospin-dependent propagation direction without altering the lattice configuration or excitation handedness remains a fundamental challenge in controlling topological edge transport. Here, we theoretically and experimentally demonstrate cavity-tunable topological edge transport in a microwave metasurface embedded within a metallic cavity. Varying the cavity height reshapes resonator interactions, driving band inversion and topological phase transition while preserving the lattice geometry. For a fixed domain-wall configuration, this transition reverses the propagation direction associated with each pseudospin while preserving pseudospin-momentum locking. Excitations with the same handedness are observed to launch edge waves propagating in opposite directions at their respective edge-band frequencies determined by the cavity height. These results establish cavity electrodynamics as an external means of controlling the bulk topology and reconfiguring the directional response of topological interfaces, creating new opportunities for topological wave routing in fixed photonic lattice structures.

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

This work advances PA-guided wavefront shaping toward a compact, same-side implementation for future biomedical therapeutic and imaging applications.

- **作者**: Cédric Rousseau, Javier González-Colsa, Victor Joris et al.

- **AI摘要**: 揭示Janus纳米加热器集体热效应的对称化机制
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Janus nanoheaters provide a route to break the spatial symmetry of thermoplasmonic heating and generate directionally biased thermal gradients at the nanoscale. However, in realistic photothermal scenarios, nanoheaters rarely act as isolated objects, but rather operate within colloidal assemblies where collective thermal and electromagnetic effects reshape the local temperature landscape. Here, we investigate how the intrinsic thermal asymmetry of a single Janus plasmonic nanoheater is transformed when moving from isolated particles to planar random arrays under continuous-wave illumination. Using coupled electromagnetic and steady-state heat-transfer simulations, we first quantify the finite spatial range over which the single-particle Janus signature remains detectable. We show that neighboring nanoheaters introduce two competing effects: a nearly symmetric collective thermal background that screens and homogenizes the Janus factor, and short-range neighbor-induced asymmetries that partially compensate this screening when interparticle distances become comparable to the spatial extent of the single-particle thermal anisotropy. We derive a closed-form analytical model for the array-averaged Janus factor as a function of nanoparticle number and surface density. The model predicts the screening-dominated regime from single-particle quantities and identifies deviations associated with near-field neighbor-induced contributions at higher densities. These results demonstrate a central trade-off for biomedical photothermal applications in continuous-wave operation: collective heating is required to reach relevant temperature increments, but it progressively erases the directional Janus character by thermal homogenization. Our framework provides a practical route to predict and rationally design the spatial temperature distribution in plasmonic assemblies of Janus particles.

- **标签**: `cond-mat.mtrl-sci` `physics.flu-dyn`
- **AI摘要**: 提出高频传质机制以解释多孔电极超极限电流
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Classical film theory predicts a finite diffusion-limited current at porous electrodes. However, over-limiting currents (OLCs), which exceed this limit, are often observed and have been attributed to surface charge, confinement, or electrokinetic effects. Here, we use a frequency-domain framework to identify a different mechanism governed by the time scale of concentration variation. For perfectly reactive interfaces, the spectral Sherwood number, a frequency-dependent dimensionless measure of interfacial mass transfer relative to molecular diffusion, grows as $\Shnd\simω^{1/2}$ at high frequency, while its phase approaches $π/4$. This response corresponds to the semi-infinite Warburg limit of the electrochemical impedance. A scaling analysis shows that the exponent and prefactor are determined by diffusion within a thin layer adjacent to the interface and are therefore independent of the global pore geometry. Numerical results for parallel plates, circular tubes, and twelve porous microstructures confirm this scaling. Geometry, flow strength, and flow direction affect the low-frequency response and crossover frequency, but not the asymptotic scaling in the high-frequency limit. This temporal route to OLCs requires neither surface charge nor confinement and could inform the design of systems for pulsed electrodeposition, mineral recovery and renewable-energy storage.
